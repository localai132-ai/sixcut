Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how SIXCUT ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the SIXCUT website and the SIXCUT Publisher application ("the App").

What the App is

SIXCUT Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by the SIXCUT team to upload SIXCUT's own videos to SIXCUT's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube using each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.

Information we access

  • Account authorization tokens that the platforms issue when the SIXCUT account owner authorizes the App. They are used only to publish videos and read basic performance numbers for SIXCUT's own posts.
  • Basic profile information of SIXCUT's own accounts (account ID, username) needed to publish to the right account.
  • Aggregate post statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of SIXCUT's own posts, used to choose better posting times.

We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal information of viewers or other users. We do not use the platforms' data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the SIXCUT team's own computer, with file access restricted to the owner. They are never sent to third parties other than the platform they belong to.

Website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time from the platform's settings (for example TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Facebook → Settings → Business integrations; Google → Third-party connections).

Children

The App and website are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @sixcut.gg on Instagram or TikTok.
